I'm Kaan Gültekin. I live in Çanakkale, and I'm a software
engineering student and a founding engineer at
[SolonGate](https://solongate.com/), where we build a security gateway for AI
agents: every tool call an agent makes is checked against a policy before it
runs.

I came to security from the offensive side. My first public repositories, in
January 2022, were small hardware and Windows security experiments. AutoPWN
Suite came out of the same months, as a way to automate the scan-then-search
loop every CTF box starts with, and by June 2022 I was ranked first in Turkey
on TryHackMe.

Looking at the list now, the projects drift from breaking things toward
building them carefully. AutoPWN Suite went from a single script to a project
with a threat model, signed releases and an OpenSSF Gold badge, and lately
the side projects are meant for other people to use: a bus app for the city I
live in, and a site that reads the monthly filings of every investment fund in
Türkiye.

## So far

- **Jan 2022.** My first public repositories: the [BadUSB proofs of concept]({{ '/projects/badusb-pocs/' | relative_url }}) and a few small Windows tools.
- **Mar 2022.** A [network scanner and a file search]({{ '/projects/powershell-projects/' | relative_url }}) in plain PowerShell, with nothing to install.
- **May 2022.** Started [AutoPWN Suite]({{ '/projects/autopwn-suite/' | relative_url }}), Nmap, NVD and Exploit-DB chained into one command.
- **Jun 2022.** [#1 in Turkey on TryHackMe](https://x.com/kaangultekin01/status/1532744189762584579), and in the global top 11 of the monthly leaderboard.
- **Oct 2022.** Wrote the AutoPWN Suite entry in PenTest Magazine's [Open-Source Pentesting Toolkit](https://legacy.pentestmag.com/download/pentest-open-source-pentesting-toolkit/) issue.
- **2022.** Listed in [Discord's Security Hall of Fame](https://web.archive.org/web/20221107033704/https://discord.com/security) for a vulnerability I reported to them privately.
- **Jun 2023.** [CompanyEnum]({{ '/projects/company-enum/' | relative_url }}), an OSINT sweep that turns a company name into a report.
- **May 2026.** A [bus app]({{ '/projects/canakkale-hat-sefer/' | relative_url }}) for my city, and [Pulsemap]({{ '/projects/pulsemap/' | relative_url }}), a packet capture you can watch.
- **Aug 2026.** [FundHunter]({{ '/projects/fundhunter/' | relative_url }}): every Turkish investment fund and what it actually holds.
- **Oct 2026.** AutoPWN Suite 2.5 to 2.9: nine releases in a month, a public security review and the OpenSSF Gold badge.
- **Now.** Founding engineer at [SolonGate](https://solongate.com/), building guardrails that check an AI agent's tool calls before they run.

## Where I draw the line

I only test systems I own or have written permission to test, and I report
what I find privately to the people who can fix it, as I did with Discord.
The offensive projects on this site are old, small and already public; each
page says what it's for and how to defend against it. Some research, like the
fileless PowerShell work, stays private on purpose.
